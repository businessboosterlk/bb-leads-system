Privacy Policy

BB Leads System — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Data Controller: Business Booster (Pvt) Ltd, Sri Lanka

1. Data We Collect

We collect lead/prospect information entered by authorized users: business names, contact persons, phone numbers, email addresses, industry, deal values, activity logs, and follow-up schedules.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used exclusively for sales pipeline management, follow-up scheduling, team coordination, and business analytics within your organization.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on Supabase (AWS infrastructure). Access is protected by PIN-based authentication mapped to Supabase Auth with Row Level Security (RLS). Sessions expire after 8 hours. All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

4. AI Features

Optional AI features use Google Gemini API. When enabled, lead context data is sent to Google's servers for processing. Your API key is stored locally in your browser. You can disable AI features at any time by removing your API key.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to: Access — export any lead's complete data. Rectification — edit any information. Erasure — permanently delete leads and all associated data. Portability — export all leads as CSV.

6. Data Retention

Lead data is retained until manually deleted. Closed-won leads are converted to clients. Closed-lost leads remain for analytics. Users can permanently delete any lead at any time, which cascades to all related activities and follow-ups.

7. Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties. Data processors: Supabase (hosting), Google (AI features, optional), GitHub Pages (app delivery).

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries: businessboosterlk@gmail.com
